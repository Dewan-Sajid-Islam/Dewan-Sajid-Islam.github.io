About Me

I'm an interdisciplinary researcher passionate about developing mathematical frameworks spanning cosmology and oncology. My work integrates theoretical physics, computational modeling, and machine learning to address both cosmic and biological systems.

My current focus includes the Scalar Unified Dark Adaptive Theory (SUDAT), high-performance cosmological simulations, and systems oncology models for multi-scale tumor analysis.

Projects

SUDAT Framework

Novel relativistic framework unifying dark matter and dark energy via baryon-coupled scalar fields. Includes derivation of field equations and perturbation spectra.

High-Performance Cosmological Simulations

Optimized MCMC pipelines in Python/C++ for efficient cosmological parameter estimation and large-scale simulation.

Systems Oncology Models

Multi-scale cancer models integrating metabolic constraints and machine learning for neoantigen prediction using TCGA datasets.

Cross-Disciplinary Methods

Applied renormalization group techniques from quantum field theory to characterize phase transitions in tumor microenvironments.
