About

I am an early‑career researcher based in Dhaka, Bangladesh, with active interests in theoretical physics, cosmology, and computational oncology. My work emphasizes mathematical consistency, physical interpretability, and cross‑disciplinary methods.

Alongside formal science education, I pursue independent research projects spanning scalar‑field cosmology, high‑performance numerical simulation, and systems‑level modeling of cancer biology. I am committed to open science and accessible dissemination of research.

A relativistic scalar‑field framework unifying dark matter and dark energy through baryon‑coupled adaptive dynamics. Submitted for peer review to Nuclear Physics B. Public preprint available; further publications forthcoming.
